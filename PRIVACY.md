# Privacy Policy — WordCompare

WordCompare is a local document comparison and merge tool.

- All document processing happens **locally on your device**. Word files
  you compare or merge never leave your machine.
- The application collects **no personal data**, performs **no telemetry
  or analytics**, and makes **no network requests**.
- There are **no accounts, no cloud sync, and no third-party data sharing**.
- Git integration runs your locally installed `git` only.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/nkunal1992/wordcompare-releases/issues
